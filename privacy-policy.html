Privacy Policy

Dream Squad Card Maker • Last updated: October 6, 2026

Dream Squad Card Maker lets users create custom football player cards and squad images. This policy explains the information used by the app and the third-party services it relies on.

Information you provide

When you create or sign in to an account, the app may process your display name, email address, profile photo, and sign-in provider information. Images that you choose for cards, logos, flags, managers, backgrounds, and squads are selected by you.

Account, app data, and coins

Firebase Authentication is used for account sign-in. Cloud Firestore is used for account-related data such as coin balance, daily reward status, rewarded-ad counters, and coin history. Card and squad project information may also be stored locally on your device so you can continue editing your work.

Photos and generated images

The app accesses photos only when you choose an image from your device. Photo editing and background-removal features are performed for the card-making experience. Generated card and squad images are saved to your device only when you choose to save or export them.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect or process device, advertising, diagnostic, and usage information according to Google's own policies. Watching a rewarded ad is optional and is used to grant in-app coins subject to the app's daily limit.

Purchases

Optional coin purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled by Google Play; the app does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Third-party services

The app uses services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sign-In, AdMob, and Google Play Billing. Those services may process information under their respective privacy policies and terms.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers needed to operate authentication, cloud data, rewarded advertising, and purchases, or when required by law.

Data retention and deletion

You can use the in-app Delete account option to request deletion of the Dream Squad account data managed by the app. Some information may also remain with third-party providers according to their own retention requirements. Local exported images remain on your device until you delete them.

Children

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that violates applicable law. Parents or guardians should supervise use where required.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect app data, but no internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The latest version will be published at this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the Dream Squad Card Maker listing in Google Play.
