Delete Your Dream Squad Card Maker Account

Dream Squad Card Maker allows users to request deletion of their account and associated account data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your account, send an email from the same email address that you use with Dream Squad Card Maker.

Use the subject: Dream Squad Account Deletion Request

Request Account Deletion
Replace YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL with the official support email address before publishing this page.

Information to include

Please include:

  • Your Dream Squad Card Maker account email address
  • A clear request to delete your account

What data will be deleted

After the deletion request is verified, we will delete account data including:

  • User account information
  • Email address associated with the account
  • User ID
  • Cloud-saved player card data, if present
  • Cloud-saved squad data, if present
  • Account coin and reward information

Data stored only on your device

Images, exported cards, or other files saved directly to your device may remain on your device after account deletion. You can remove these files manually from your device.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are normally processed after the account owner has been verified.
